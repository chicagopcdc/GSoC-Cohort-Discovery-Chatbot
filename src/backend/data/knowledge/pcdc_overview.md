## What PCDC is

PCDC stands for Pediatric Cancer Data Commons. It is the flagship data commons project of Data for the Common Good (D4CG) at the University of Chicago. PCDC brings pediatric, adolescent and young adult, and adult cancer clinical research data into a harmonized platform so researchers can explore cohorts and assess study feasibility across multiple cancer types.

## What the PCDC Data Portal is for

The PCDC Data Portal lets researchers explore available harmonized clinical data, use cohort exploration and analysis tools, and prepare for research project requests. It is designed for research use. Line-level data access is governed by the portal's data access and project request process.

## Data dictionaries

PCDC uses disease-based data dictionaries and consensus data standards. These dictionaries define how clinical data elements are represented, mapped, and harmonized across contributing studies and consortia.
